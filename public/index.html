How it works

Film. Fix. Keep the money.

i.

Film the problem

Ten to thirty seconds on your phone. FixFrame marks exactly what's wrong, with timestamps you can click.

ii.

Get the exact part

Every fault becomes a real part from real stores via Channel3. You can even search by the photo of the worn piece.

iii.

Fix it, or hire it

Follow steps pinned to your own video, or book a pro. PayPal holds the money until the fix is verified.
